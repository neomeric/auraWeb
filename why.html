The Why

Hiring has been broken for
50 years. We decided to fix it.

Not with another ATS. Not with another scheduling tool. Not with AI that writes job descriptions nobody reads.

We built the thing that was missing — the ability to understand what actually happens in an interview. Beneath the words. Beyond the performance. Past the rehearsed answers.

"The most important signal in every interview is the one nobody can see."

The Science

Three signals. One truth.

Human emotion doesn't hide. It leaks through the face, the voice, and the words — all at once. We read all three.

Face

Micro-expressions that last 1/25th of a second. Confidence, doubt, stress, surprise — detected in real-time from video. The face reveals what words hide.

Voice

Pitch, pace, pauses, tremor. The voice carries emotional weight no transcript can capture. We hear what the ear misses.

Words

Sentiment, certainty, hedging, specificity. The language patterns that separate genuine expertise from rehearsed answers.

These three signals are fused into a single emotional portrait — the complete picture, not just a piece of it.
